## Understanding Custom Pallets and Skids

Custom pallets and skids are designed according to the specific requirements of a business. Unlike standard pallets with fixed dimensions, custom solutions can be built with different sizes, materials, load capacities, and structural designs. These options allow manufacturers to create packaging that fits their products securely while improving warehouse and shipping efficiency.

A skid is similar to a pallet but typically does not include bottom deck boards. Skids are often used for heavy equipment and industrial applications where products need strong base support and easy movement with forklifts or other handling equipment. Custom pallets, on the other hand, are commonly used for storage, transportation, and distribution.

## Consider Product Size and Weight Requirements

One of the most important factors when selecting custom pallets and skids is understanding the size and weight of the products being transported. A properly designed pallet must support the product without bending, breaking, or becoming unstable during handling.

Businesses should consider:

- Product dimensions
- Total load weight
- Weight distribution
- Transportation methods
- Storage conditions

For heavy industrial products, reinforced pallets and skids may be necessary to provide additional strength. A professional pallet manufacturer can evaluate these factors and recommend the right design for safe and efficient handling.

High-quality lumber helps create pallets that withstand repeated handling and transportation challenges. Depending on the application, businesses may require hardwood or softwood options, treated materials, or specialized construction techniques.
