## The 48×40 GMA Pallet — Hartford’s Most Ordered Standard

If your business operates in grocery, retail, consumer goods, or general distribution, the 48×40 GMA pallet is almost certainly already part of your supply chain. Established by the Grocery Manufacturers Association, this sizing became the universal standard for North American logistics and remains the most widely specified pallet dimension across industries far beyond its grocery origins.

Here’s why 48×40 GMA pallets Hartford CT businesses order consistently and in volume:

- **Universal compatibility** — Fits standard warehouse racking, dock levelers, and retail floor displays across virtually every facility layout
- **Four-way forklift access** — Allows handling from any direction, speeding up loading and unloading significantly
- **Trailer space optimization** — The 48×40 footprint maximizes utilization inside standard 53-foot freight trailers
- **Strong load capacity** — Properly graded GMA pallets support approximately 2,500 lbs static and 1,500 lbs dynamic load
- **Retailer compliance** — Major retail chains and big-box stores mandate GMA pallet sizing for all vendor deliveries
- **Easy repair and remanufacture** — GMA pallets are the most commonly repaired format, extending usable life and reducing per-unit cost

Whether you’re supplying a Hartford distribution center, fulfilling retail vendor orders, or managing high-volume inbound freight, GMA pallets are the baseline specification your operation most likely already works around.
