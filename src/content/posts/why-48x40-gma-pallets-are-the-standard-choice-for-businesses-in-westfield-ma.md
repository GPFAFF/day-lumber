## Understanding the Importance of GMA Pallets

GMA (Grocery Manufacturers Association) pallets are designed around a standard 48-inch by 40-inch footprint. This universal sizing has made them one of the most widely accepted pallet formats in North America. Many industries use these pallets because they work seamlessly with common forklifts, pallet jacks, warehouse racks, and shipping equipment.
