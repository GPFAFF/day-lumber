## Understanding Export Pallets and ISPM 15 Requirements

Export pallets are specially prepared wooden pallets used for transporting goods across international borders. Many countries require wood packaging materials to meet ISPM 15 standards, which help prevent the spread of insects and pests through untreated wood products.

Businesses looking for export pallets ISPM 15 near me Chicopee MA should choose pallets that have undergone approved treatment processes. ISPM 15 compliance typically requires heat treatment, which involves heating wood to a specific temperature for a set period to eliminate harmful organisms.

Using compliant export pallets helps businesses avoid customs issues, shipment delays, and rejected loads. Before purchasing pallets for international shipping, companies should confirm that the pallets include proper markings showing they meet ISPM 15 requirements.
