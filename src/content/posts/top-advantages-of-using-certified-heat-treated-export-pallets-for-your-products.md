## What Are Certified Heat Treated Export Pallets?

Certified heat treated export pallets are wooden pallets that undergo a specialized treatment process to eliminate harmful pests and organisms found in raw wood. These pallets meet the requirements of the International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM 15), which regulates the movement of wood packaging materials across international borders.

The heat treatment process involves heating wood pallets to a specific internal temperature for a required period. Once completed, the pallets receive an official certification mark showing they meet international shipping requirements. This certification helps reduce the risk of customs delays and ensures compliance with export regulations.
