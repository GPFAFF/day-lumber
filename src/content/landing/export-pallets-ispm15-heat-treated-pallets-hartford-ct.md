### ISPM 15 Export Pallets for International Shipping

When businesses export goods internationally, they must comply with **ISPM 15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15)** regulations. These standards govern how wood packaging materials are treated before entering global markets.

Companies searching for ISPM 15 pallets in Hartford CT typically require pallets that have been heat treated and certified according to these international guidelines. The purpose of ISPM 15 compliance is to prevent the spread of pests, insects, and plant diseases that may exist in untreated wood.

During the manufacturing process, pallets are placed in controlled heat treatment chambers where the wood is heated to a specific core temperature for a set duration. This process eliminates harmful organisms and ensures the wood is safe for international transport.

Using ISPM 15-compliant pallets helps businesses avoid customs delays, shipment rejection, or quarantine inspections. For exporters in Hartford, certified pallets are essential for maintaining smooth and efficient international logistics operations.

## Heat Treated Pallets for Global Compliance

Businesses preparing for export often rely on heat treated pallets to meet ISPM 15 requirements. Heat treatment is the most widely accepted method for ensuring wooden pallets comply with international shipping regulations.

In this process, pallets are treated in specialized kilns where the internal temperature of the wood is raised to eliminate pests and microorganisms. Once completed, pallets receive an official **HT (Heat Treated)** stamp, confirming they meet global compliance standards.

This certification is recognized by customs authorities worldwide, making heat treated pallets suitable for use in container shipping, air freight, and international cargo transportation. By using certified pallets, businesses can reduce the risk of delays and ensure smoother movement of goods across borders.
