### ISPM 15 Certified Pallets for International Shipping

ISPM 15 regulations are established to prevent the spread of pests and contaminants through wood packaging materials used in global trade. Pallets that meet this standard undergo a controlled treatment process and are marked to indicate compliance.

Using certified pallets helps businesses avoid customs delays, shipment rejections, and additional inspection costs. For companies exporting goods, ISPM 15 compliance is essential for maintaining smooth and uninterrupted logistics operations.

## Heat Treated Pallets for Safety and Compliance

Heat treated pallets are widely used in both domestic and international shipping due to their reliability and compliance benefits. During the treatment process, pallets are heated to a specific internal temperature that eliminates insects and microorganisms without affecting structural strength.

These pallets are suitable for a variety of applications, including warehouse storage, freight handling, and global shipping. Their consistent construction ensures dependable load support while maintaining compatibility with forklifts, pallet jacks, and racking systems.

Using heat treated pallets helps businesses meet regulatory requirements while protecting goods throughout the supply chain.
