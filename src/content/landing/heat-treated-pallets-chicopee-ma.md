## Heat Treated Pallets for Safe Shipping

Heat treated pallets are widely used for both domestic and international shipping. During the treatment process, wood is heated to a specific internal temperature to eliminate insects, larvae, and contaminants.

Once treated, pallets are stamped with an official **HT (Heat Treated)** mark, confirming compliance with international standards. This certification allows pallets to be used confidently in export shipments and ensures they meet global regulatory requirements.

In addition to compliance, heat treated pallets offer reliable strength and durability. They are designed to withstand heavy loads, repeated handling, and long-distance transportation, making them ideal for warehouse and logistics operations.
