### ISPM 15 Certified Export Pallets for Global Shipping

Export pallets must meet the standards set under ISPM 15 regulations, which are designed to prevent the spread of pests and contaminants through wooden packaging materials. These pallets undergo a heat treatment process that eliminates insects and microorganisms, ensuring compliance with international shipping requirements.

Using ISPM 15 certified pallets helps businesses avoid shipment delays, customs rejections, and additional inspection costs. For companies shipping goods overseas, compliant pallets are essential for maintaining efficient and uninterrupted logistics operations.

## Heat Treated Pallets for Compliance and Durability

Heat treated pallets are widely used in both domestic and international shipping because they provide a balance of compliance, durability, and safety. During the treatment process, pallets are exposed to controlled temperatures that eliminate pests without compromising the structural integrity of the wood.

These pallets are suitable for a wide range of applications, including warehouse storage, freight transportation, and export shipments. Their consistent construction ensures reliable load support while maintaining compatibility with forklifts, pallet jacks, and racking systems.

By using heat treated pallets, businesses can reduce the risk of contamination while ensuring their shipments meet regulatory standards across different regions.
