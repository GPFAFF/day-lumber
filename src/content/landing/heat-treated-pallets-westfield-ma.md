### ISPM 15 Certified Export Pallets

ISPM 15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) is a global regulation that governs wood packaging materials used in international trade. Its primary purpose is to prevent the spread of pests and invasive species through untreated wood.

To comply with these standards, pallets must undergo a treatment process—typically heat treatment—and be marked with a certification stamp. This stamp confirms that the pallet meets international requirements and is approved for export use.

Businesses using ISPM 15-certified pallets benefit from smoother customs clearance, reduced risk of shipment rejection, and improved reliability in global shipping operations.

## Heat Treated Pallets for International and Domestic Use

Heat treated pallets are widely used in both domestic and international logistics. During the treatment process, wood is heated to a specific internal temperature to eliminate insects, larvae, and contaminants.

Once treated, pallets receive an official **HT (Heat Treated)** mark, indicating compliance with international standards. This certification is recognized worldwide and allows pallets to be used confidently in export shipments.

In addition to compliance, heat treated pallets offer improved durability and stability. They perform reliably in warehouse environments, shipping containers, and long-distance transportation, making them a practical solution for businesses managing complex supply chains.
