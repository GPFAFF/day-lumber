### Understanding ISPM 15 Export Requirements

ISPM 15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) regulates wood packaging materials used in international trade. The purpose of this standard is to prevent the spread of pests and plant diseases that can be transported through untreated wood.

To comply with ISPM 15, pallets must undergo a treatment process that eliminates insects and contaminants. Heat treatment is the most widely used method, after which pallets are marked to indicate compliance.

Businesses in New Haven benefit from using ISPM 15-compliant pallets because they are accepted by most countries, ensuring smoother customs clearance and minimizing the risk of shipment delays.

## Heat Treated Pallets for International Shipping

Heat treated pallets are essential for export logistics because they meet global compliance standards while maintaining structural strength. During the treatment process, wood is heated to a specific internal temperature to eliminate pests, larvae, and microorganisms.

Once treated, pallets receive an official **HT (Heat Treated)** stamp, confirming they meet international shipping requirements. This certification is recognized worldwide and allows pallets to be used in global transportation networks without restrictions.

These pallets are commonly used in shipping containers, freight systems, and distribution operations, providing both compliance and durability for international shipments.
