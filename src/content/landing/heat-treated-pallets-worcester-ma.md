### Understanding ISPM 15 Export Requirements

ISPM 15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) regulates wood packaging materials used in international shipping. The purpose of this standard is to prevent the spread of pests and plant diseases that can be transported through untreated wood.

To meet ISPM 15 requirements, pallets must undergo a treatment process that eliminates insects and contaminants. Most commonly, this is done through heat treatment, after which pallets are stamped to indicate compliance.

Businesses in Worcester that use ISPM 15-compliant pallets benefit from smoother customs clearance and reduced risk of shipment delays. Certified pallets are accepted in most countries, making them essential for global trade operations.

## Heat Treated Pallets for International Shipping

Heat treated pallets are widely used in export logistics because they meet international compliance requirements while maintaining structural strength. During the treatment process, wood is heated to a specific internal temperature to eliminate pests, larvae, and microorganisms.

Once treated, pallets receive an official **HT (Heat Treated)** stamp, confirming they meet global standards. This certification is recognized worldwide and ensures pallets can be used for international shipments without restrictions.

Businesses rely on heat treated pallets for transporting goods through shipping containers, freight systems, and global distribution networks. These pallets provide both compliance and durability, making them ideal for export operations.
