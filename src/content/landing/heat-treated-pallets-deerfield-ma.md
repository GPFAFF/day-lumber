### ISPM 15 Certified Export Pallets

ISPM 15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) is a global regulation governing wood packaging materials used in international trade. Its purpose is to prevent the spread of pests and invasive species through untreated wood.

To meet these requirements, pallets must undergo a treatment process—most commonly heat treatment—and receive a certification mark indicating compliance. Businesses using ISPM 15-certified pallets benefit from smoother customs clearance and reduced risk of shipment delays or rejections.

By choosing compliant pallets, companies ensure their goods meet international standards while maintaining consistent quality and reliability during shipping.

## Heat Treated Pallets for Global Shipping

Heat treated pallets are widely used for both domestic and international shipping. During the treatment process, wood is heated to a specific internal temperature to eliminate insects, fungi, and other contaminants.

Once treated, pallets are marked with an official **HT (Heat Treated)** stamp, confirming compliance with international shipping standards. This certification is recognized globally and allows pallets to be used in export operations without restrictions.

Heat treated pallets provide strong structural support, making them suitable for warehouse storage, material handling, and long-distance transportation. Businesses benefit from improved product protection and reduced risk of contamination.
